## Netflix Movie Analysis
In this Project I performed EDA on Netflix Data to uncover the patterns and trends present in the data .
## Workflow -
Step 1 - Importing the Necessary Libraries to the Jupyter Notebook.
Step 2 - Creating Pandas Dataframe from the given data and performed the required steps for initial understanding and exploration of data.
Step 3 - Cleaning and removing null and duplicate values to avoid inconsistency from the data
Step 4 - Univariate and Bivariate analysis of the data
Step 5 - Creating visualizations from Matplotlib and Seaborn Libraries.

## Overview of Project -
Answered Below Questions from our analysis
1. What is the most frequent genre of movies released on Netflix?
2. Which has highest votes in vote avg column?
3. What movie got the highest popularity? what's its genre?
4. What movie got the lowest popularity? what's its genre?
5. Which year has the most filmmed movies?
